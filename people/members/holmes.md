# Alexander Holmes
[![]({{site.baseurl}}/images/Holmes.jpg){: .left .round .small}](/members/holmes.html)

| **Postdoctoral Researcher** |  
| Alex completed his PhD in Australia with Prof. Alex Fornito and Dr. James Pang, where he investigated the developmental, behavioral, and clinical aspects of hierarchical cortical function. Since joining the lab at Oxford, he has continued to model the functional and geometric modes of the cerebral cortex, while also integrating cross-species and genetic perspectives towards the guiding organisational principles of the human brain. |
